# Reel story: "The Great Fruit Adventure" (Video 1: Fruits in 3 languages)

Vertical 9:16 (1080 x 1920). About 80 seconds total: 5 s intro + 10 fruit scenes of about 6-7 s + 5 s finale.
You can also post each fruit scene as its own 8-12 s reel (a series).

## The story in one minute
Three friends (Bonbon the bunny, Churro the bear, Milo the penguin) find a treasure map to the Magical Fruit Forest.
They must fill the big Rainbow Basket for a picnic party. Each stop is a different place (orchard, jungle, garden, vineyard, beach clearing)
where they harvest a fruit from its plant, with a silly accident every time. Each fruit ends with a "reveal" moment where each friend
holds the fruit up and says its name in their own language (English / French / Spanish). At the end all ten fruits are in the basket and they party.

Setting for every scene: a bright, magical forest/jungle, lush leaves, flowers, butterflies, ladybugs, sunbeams, big friendly plants. No plain blue background.
Style: colorful flat 2D cartoon for toddlers, thick soft outlines, huge glossy eyes, rosy cheeks, bright cheerful colors, gentle gradients, super cute. Nothing scary.

## 2. GLOBAL SETTINGS
- Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical.
- Add to every image prompt: `no text, no letters, no logos, same characters and outfits as the reference image, not scary, not realistic`
- Negative prompt (if the tool has one): `text, watermark, extra characters, realistic, 3D render, scary, dark, blurry, deformed hands, changed hats, missing beret, missing sombrero, missing cap`
- Do NOT ask the AI to draw words (it misspells them). Add the English / French / Spanish words as text in your editor (colors: English red, French blue, Spanish yellow).
- Workflow: 1) make the still image for the scene, 2) pick the best, 3) animate it with an image-to-video tool using the ANIMATION prompt, 4) 4-6 second clips, 5) assemble in CapCut or Canva, add voices, music, words.
- Keep one rule for animation prompts: "gentle cartoon motion, characters stay on model, camera mostly still, no morphing".

## 4. REVEAL STRUCTURE for each fruit scene (about 6-7 seconds)
0.0-3.0 s: harvest action and gag (no words yet)
3.0-4.0 s: freeze-frame or slow zoom, English word appears (red), Milo says it
4.0-5.2 s: French word appears (blue), Bonbon says it
5.2-6.5 s: Spanish word appears (yellow), Churro says it
Optional: quiz after fruit 5 and fruit 10 ("What is it?", 3 seconds pause, then the three words).

## 5. SOUND
- Light, playful ukulele or marimba loop (royalty-free, check the license).
- SFX per gag: boing (pear bonk), slide whistle (banana slip), pop (pineapple), sour "wheee" (lemon), sparkle (cherry), soft thud (apple).
- Voices: see the recording script file (record yourself or a voice actor).

## 6. QUALITY CHECKLIST (before using a clip)
- All three characters present and in the correct outfits (beret, sombrero, cap).
- No wrong fruit, no extra animals, no weird hands.
- The harvest shows the fruit on its real plant (tree, vine, bush or ground).
- Funny but gentle, nothing scary.
- Add words yourself in the editor, never trust the AI with text.
