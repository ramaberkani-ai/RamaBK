## How to animate each still for free (on your laptop)
1. Open the Canva link below (it opens the image in your Canva account).
2. Download it at full size (Share > Download > PNG), or use it directly in Canva.
3. Animate it with ONE of these, pasting the MOTION prompt:
   - Canva "Image to Video" (free plan, allowance varies; 5 s clips; subtle motion). https://www.canva.com/help/image-to-video/
   - Pavo AI (reported free daily credits, 720p image-to-video). Check watermark and terms in the app.
   - Google Vids (about 10 free Veo generations a month, reported). Use it for 1 or 2 hero scenes (finale, watermelon).
   - PixVerse (reported 60 free credits a day, watermark on free exports). Test only.
4. Save each clip as 01_intro.mp4, 02_apple.mp4 ... 12_finale.mp4 and send them to me.
5. I will assemble them (ffmpeg, free): freeze-frame for the word reveal, colored subtitles
   (English red, French blue, Spanish yellow), an .srt file, and gaps for your voices.

Tip: do 3-4 clips a day if a tool gives daily credits. Regenerate a clip if a character loses an accessory.

## Rule for every motion prompt
Add at the end: "gentle cartoon motion, characters stay on model, same outfits, camera mostly still, no morphing, no text".
